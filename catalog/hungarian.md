### HungarianPod101

*The biggest structured Hungarian audio course, publishing daily through 2026.*

course, podcast, app · A1–B1 · Freemium · listening, vocabulary, grammar

Hundreds of recorded dialogues with line-by-line breakdowns, vocabulary lists, flashcards and PDF notes, plus a YouTube channel that was posting in August 2026 — for a language this size, that volume of professionally recorded beginner listening is hard to find elsewhere. Its weakness is the familiar Pod101 one: lessons are grouped into pathways but do not build tightly on each other, so it supplements a course better than it replaces one. The free tier is limited and the upsell pressure is constant.

### HungarianReference.com

*Free grammar reference that finally makes Hungarian's ~20 cases feel systematic.*

website, book · A1–C1 · Free · grammar, pronunciation, reading

Daniel Clarke's site explains Hungarian grammar for English speakers as an interlocking system — vowel harmony, the case suffixes, possession, definite versus indefinite conjugation — rather than as a list of rules to memorise, which is exactly what most learners need when Hungarian first looks impossible. It also covers the alphabet and pronunciation, so it works from day one. Completely free with no account. It is a reference, not a course: no exercises, no audio programme, and you will need input material alongside it.

[Website](https://hungarianreference.com/)

### r/hungarian

*Learner community where natives untangle suffixes and word order questions.*

community · Free · writing, grammar, vocabulary, reading

The main English-speaking gathering point for Hungarian learners, where native speakers explain why a suffix chain or word order sounds wrong — the kind of question Hungarian generates constantly and that references answer slowly. Recurring threads also collect resources, which matters for a language with a thin published market. Free. Traffic is moderate, so replies take hours, and answers occasionally conflict when native intuition varies by region.

[Website](https://www.reddit.com/r/hungarian/)

### Telex

*Independent Hungarian news site — daily authentic reading once you reach B2.*

media · B2–C2 · Free · reading, vocabulary, immersion

One of Hungary's main independent news outlets, reader-funded and free, publishing across politics, culture, science and long-form features. The range of registers is what makes it useful: a culture feature and a news brief demand very different vocabulary from the same site. Free. This is unadapted native writing with heavy use of verbal prefixes and compound nouns, so it belongs at B2 and above rather than as early reading practice.

[Website](https://telex.hu/) · [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/telex/id1554485847) · [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=hu.app.telex) · [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/c/telexponthu)

### Debrecen Summer School

*Hungary's oldest school of Hungarian for foreigners — summer intensives and online courses.*

course, tutoring · A1–C1 · Paid · speaking, grammar, listening, writing

Founded in 1927 and still the best-known institution teaching Hungarian to foreigners, running intensive summer programmes in Debrecen plus year-round classroom and online courses, individually or in groups, with culture-focused thematic options. An intensive month here does more than a year of casual app use, which is the honest case for paying. Courses are paid, with prices depending on format and length. The site is largely in Hungarian, which is an odd hurdle for prospective beginners.

[Website](https://nyariegyetem.hu/)

### DictZone

*Free English–Hungarian dictionary with 650k+ pairs, examples and audio.*

dictionary · A1–C2 · Free · vocabulary, reading, writing, pronunciation

The largest free English-Hungarian dictionary on the web, with example sentences, synonyms, phonetic transcriptions, audio pronunciation and grammatical information including conjugations and plurals. For everyday lookups while reading it is faster and more complete than app dictionaries. Free. Entries are partly user-submitted, so quality is uneven at the edges — cross-check anything unusual against a monolingual source before relying on it.

[Website](https://dictzone.com/angol-magyar-szotar/)

### Hungarian: An Essential Grammar

*Carol Rounds' reference grammar — the standard English-language Hungarian one.*

book · B1–C2 · Paid · grammar, writing

The most widely used English-language description of Hungarian grammar, systematic about the case system, verbal prefixes, definite and indefinite conjugation and word order in a way no free site fully matches. It is organised for lookup, assumes basic grammatical terminology, and rewards you most once you are past the beginner stage and asking precise questions. Now in its second edition. Paid, and dense — it explains thoroughly but will not entertain you.

[Website](https://www.routledge.com/Hungarian-An-Essential-Grammar/Rounds/p/book/9780415777377)

### Live Lingua Hungarian (FSI & Peace Corps)

*Fourteen public-domain government courses: 23 ebooks and 279 audio files, free.*

course, book · A1–B2 · Free · grammar, reading, listening, vocabulary

Mirrors the public-domain US government (FSI and Peace Corps) Hungarian materials — 14 courses, 23 ebooks and 279 audio files at no cost, which is by far the largest free structured archive for Hungarian. The FSI method is drill-heavy and unusually rigorous about morphology, which suits Hungarian's suffix system well. The recordings are decades old, so audio quality is poor and some vocabulary is dated. Free, though the site pushes its paid tutoring alongside.

[Website](https://www.livelingua.com/courses/hungarian)

### WikiSzótár

*Monolingual Hungarian explanatory dictionary with etymology and example sentences.*

dictionary · B1–C2 · Free · vocabulary, reading

A Hungarian-to-Hungarian explanatory dictionary giving meanings, word origins, grammatical notes and example sentences — the step to take once translating every word back to English starts holding you back. Etymology entries are genuinely interesting in Hungarian, where word-building is so transparent. Free to use. Entirely in Hungarian with no English interface, so realistically it becomes usable around B1.

[Website](https://wikiszotar.hu/)
